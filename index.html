Selected work

Client details are withheld under confidentiality agreements. References and detailed scope are available on request.

Public-sector housing finance agency

Automating mortgage origination and external lender integration

A quasi-governmental housing finance agency was processing mortgage applications through a workflow that depended on manual data entry and re-keying between its own loan systems and those of an external banking partner. We designed and delivered robotic process automation with AI-based document and data handling to automate the origination steps, and built a systems integration that moved information between the partner bank and the agency's internal processes without human intervention.

Regulated public-sector environment. Detailed references available on request under NDA.
Apparel & consumer goods manufacturing

Getting orders, inventory and fulfilment out of incompatible systems

Order and fulfilment data for the business's retail accounts was split across systems that could not talk to each other, so staff re-keyed it by hand and reconciled the differences afterwards. We built RPA and AI automation to connect those flows directly to the retailers' platforms, so orders, inventory and fulfilment data moved between the manufacturer and its retail partners without manual intervention. The work was delivered following the consolidation of two major manufacturing businesses, across several of the largest retail chains in North America.

Delivered during an active post-merger integration across multiple business units.
